# Code of Conduct

RestBar follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work. Harassment and personal attacks aren't welcome here, in any form.

To report a problem, use [GitHub's report feature](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) or contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Noxr3). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove content or block people who break these rules.
